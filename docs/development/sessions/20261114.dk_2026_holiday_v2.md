# De Kosnik holiday 2026, version 2

**Superseded for the full set:** the user requested a reset into
[`dk-2026-holiday-v3`](20261114.dk_2026_holiday_v3.md), with more asymmetric
layouts, more whitespace, notan and more vibrant hues. The v2 full batch was
stopped; its visual trials and human corrections are preserved here. The
current full-set target is v3, not completion of the earlier v2 composition.

## Current brief

Create **`dk-2026-holiday-v2`** from the same five complete photographs in
`resources.rizal/de-kosnik-2026`. Produce **ten variations per photograph,
fifty designs in total**. This carries forward the per-photograph scope of
v1, expanded from four to ten versions.

Every design uses the same **6 x 4 inch landscape frame**: **152.4 x 101.6 mm**.
The photographic region must occupy **no more than 50% of the total frame
area**, measured from its full rectangular bounds before fading. **Updated
by human review: the ceiling is now 61%, superseding the original 50%.** Preserve the
whole source photograph and its aspect ratio, including the portrait-oriented
facade photograph. Do not substitute isolated faces or face crops.

The photo must **fade into the guilloche**. The ornament should grow out of
the photo's composition, gestures and background. Remove the v1 treatment of
a strongly bounded rectangular photo plate surrounded by an ornamental frame.
The desired complexity is the **wild, continuous, intersecting guilloche of
the last successful stage-3 renderings**: interference, displaced lenses,
warped sheets, membranes and dense changes of direction across the card.

The portrait API still supplies **DLH: dot / line / cross-hatch**. Prefer
strong contrast and vary mixture and flow across the ten studies. **The
photographic engraving must also be colored**: its dots, primary cuts,
cross-hatching and feature contours share the guilloche palette. Black is
not the default portrait color. Preserve readability through color contrast
against the paper or dark ground.

## Ten palette variations

Each photograph receives exactly two versions from each requested group.
The eight explicitly themed palettes expand the earlier photo-only palette
restriction. The first two continue to infer their colors from the photo.

| Versions | Requested group | Planned direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v01-v02 | Two inferred, high-contrast schemes | Photo-derived chromatic inks on a pale ground; a dark-ground counterpart with bright source-derived accents |
| v03-v04 | Two Japanese palette schemes | Japanese-inspired indigo / vermilion / ivory; ink-blue / persimmon / muted moss and warm paper |
| v05-v06 | Two neon schemes | Acid yellow-green / hot magenta; ultraviolet / electric cyan with bright accents on dark grounds |
| v07-v08 | Two blue / white / creme schemes | Cobalt on porcelain and creme; midnight blue with pale blue, white and creme |
| v09-v10 | Two red / green / black schemes | Vermilion and forest green; black-ground holly green and vivid red |

The Japanese schemes are authored color interpretations, not claims to
reproduce a named historical object's exact pigment measurements. Source
samples and derived palette values will be recorded. Keep the two members of
each group visibly different in color balance and field behavior.

## Proposed DLH and flow recipes

These are starting recipes for visual trials, to be refined if necessary.
They are combined exploratory variations, not a factorial experiment.
Contrast starts at **1.65** in every version; contour emphasis starts at 0.45.
DLH values are relative tone weights, not measured percentages of marks.

| Version | DLH | Flow follow | Maximum turn, radians | Field tension, mm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| v01 | 65 / 25 / 10 | 0.18 | 0.035 | 7 |
| v02 | 15 / 65 / 20 | 0.88 | 0.160 | 10 |
| v03 | 35 / 45 / 20 | 0.25 | 0.040 | 8 |
| v04 | 15 / 20 / 65 | 0.55 | 0.100 | 11 |
| v05 | 10 / 75 / 15 | 0.95 | 0.200 | 12 |
| v06 | 33 / 33 / 33 | 0.05 | 0.010 | 14 |
| v07 | 70 / 20 / 10 | 0.12 | 0.020 | 8 |
| v08 | 20 / 25 / 55 | 0.35 | 0.060 | 12 |
| v09 | 25 / 60 / 15 | 0.80 | 0.140 | 11 |
| v10 | 33 / 33 / 33 | 0.00 | 0.000 | 15 |

The last recipe retains the high-contrast, low-curve direction. Source
feature contours and the guilloche field remain independently curved; zero
flow and turn constrain the native photo cuts. All ten use mixed DLH.

At the smaller photographic size, start with 0.25 mm rows, 0.055-0.16 mm
line widths, a 0.20 mm maximum dot diameter and 3.5 mm maximum cut length.
Generate geometry at its final physical size rather than shrinking a v1 SVG.

## Whole-photo placement and transition

- Landscape sources: begin with **98 x 73.5 mm**, approximately **46.52%** of
  the 6 x 4 inch frame area. Move the region to suit the scene without
  enlarging it beyond the limit. Under the revised 61% ceiling, the bolder
  variants use **112 x 84 mm**, approximately **60.76%**. v01, v03 and v07
  retain the smaller comparison size; H01-v03's accepted layout is preserved.
- Portrait source H02: begin with **60 x 80 mm**, approximately **31.00%** of
  the frame area, within the same landscape card. Use the remaining lateral
  space for the facade's expanding perspective field and lettering. Bolder
  H02 variants use **69 x 92 mm**, approximately **41.00%**, keeping the full
  portrait-oriented image inside the landscape card.
- Keep the entire EXIF-oriented source. Native input support remains a full
  rectangle; the edge transition is a composition treatment after engraving.
- Fade photographic mark color/opacity progressively near the full image
  boundary. Avoid a visible rectangular paper reserve or outline around it.
- Reduce ornament through the photograph's central readable areas, then
  increase it through the fading edge. The source and guilloche should share
  one continuous surface, with no abrupt mask-shaped transition.
- Apply palette colors to DLH layers, with contrast adjusted for the ground.
  Preserve the same shapes and source-derived tonal structure while changing
  paint. Keep lettering clear of important photographed gestures.

not satisfy this brief.
