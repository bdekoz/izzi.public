# Anniversary 24, stage4: holiday-v3 portrait settings

The user requested this after completion of holiday-v3: re-render **just the
portrait parts** of `guilloche-anniversary-24-stage3` with the portrait settings
used in holiday-v3, and put the result in `guilloche-anniversary-24-stage4`.

## Scope and decisions

The series retains all **twenty front/back pairs, forty faces and forty-eight
portrait placements**. Source selections, crops, authored membership masks,
photographed coverings and physical portrait sizes remain the stage3 inputs.
The original canvas remains **155.956 × 66.294 mm (6.14 × 2.61 inches)**, with
3 mm bleed. The holiday series' 6 × 4 format and full-photo layouts do not
replace the anniversary layout.

The renderer reads the existing stage3 SVGs and replaces only the nested
native portrait groups. Everything outside those groups is preserved,
including guilloche paths, paper, reserve silhouettes, transforms, lettering,
numeral treatment and borders. A structural digest verifies this for each
face. Stage3 output files remain unchanged.

The ten holiday-v3 DLH/flow recipes cycle across v01–v10 and repeat across
v11–v20. Both sides and both portraits of a twin composition use their card's
recipe. The original deterministic anniversary seed remains in use. DLH
values are relative tone weights, not final mark-count percentages.

Shared portrait settings match holiday-v3: contrast **1.65**, brightness
0.015, gamma 1, contour emphasis **0.45**, row pitch **0.25 mm**, cut width
**0.055–0.16 mm**, maximum dot diameter **0.20 mm**, maximum cut length
**3.5 mm**, taper 0.25, highlight/shadow thresholds 0.18/0.46, crossing angle
1.12 radians, feature suppression 0, tensor radius/sigma 12/5, tone smoothing
0.12, analysis long edge 800 and coverage sampling 0.05 mm. Black and white
levels remain the source mask's 1.5th/98.5th percentiles.

All stage3 portraits sit on pale reserves, even on the noir cards, so they
use dark ink with positive source luminance. The dark holiday cards' inverted
light-ink preparation is unnecessary for these reserves. Portrait cuts take
the original field hue, dots and contours the hot accent, and shadow cuts
the ink hue. Lightness is adjusted for at least 4.5:1 contrast against the
reserve while preserving hue. The surrounding palette is unchanged.

The holiday rectangle-edge fade is a whole-photo composition treatment;
these anniversary portraits retain their existing shaped support and reserve.
Native geometry is generated directly at each original physical width, then
colored without changing any mark shape.

## Delivery

Output: `outputs/guilloche-anniversary-24-stage4/`.

- Forty SVG masters, 600 dpi PNGs and browser previews.
- Twenty paired vector review pages, forty vector trim pages and forty
  vector bleed pages, with explicit trim/bleed boxes.
- Gallery, two contact sheets and a combined overview.
- Native portrait geometry, diagnostics, source and recipe receipts.
- Exact preservation checks, independent native replays and PDF comparisons.

The source/recipe records and durable validation reports live in
`examples.data/guilloche-anniversary-24-stage4/`.

```sh
python3 scripts/render-guilloche-anniversary-24-stage4.py --deliver
python3 scripts/check-guilloche-anniversary-24-stage4.py --replay
python3 scripts/review-guilloche-anniversary-24-stage4.py
```

## Progress

Holiday-v3 finished first, with fifty designs, 2,300 checks, fifty matching
native replays and all fifty print pages checked at 600 dpi. Its complete
record is [here](20261114.dk_2026_holiday_v3.md).

Stage4 is **digitally complete**. Trials covered v01, v02, v06 and v10 before
the full forty-face batch. All SVGs, 600 dpi PNGs, previews, contact sheets,
gallery and three vector PDFs are delivered.

**1,729 checks and all 28 unique native portrait replays passed.** Those 28
geometries serve the 48 placements. Every original stage3 SVG hash still
matches, and every SVG element outside the native portrait groups is
structurally identical. All input plane bytes, source crops, masks, physical
sizes and placement transforms match their stage3 predecessors. Every
resolved native control matches the corresponding rendered holiday-v3 recipe.

All forty trim PDF pages were independently rendered at 600 dpi and compared
with the SVG PNGs. Using stage3's comparison method (common-origin crop of
at most one extra edge pixel, Gaussian sigma 0.6), maximum normalized RGB MAE
is **0.0012431223**, below 0.025. Maximum unblurred MAE is **0.0015009399**.
Trim, bleed and review PDFs contain vector artwork with no embedded rasters;
all page dimensions and bleed trim boxes pass.

All twenty paired PDF pages were inspected through five four-page montages;
the first two were also inspected individually. Both contact sheets,
representative enlarged previews and bleed pages 1/40 were inspected. Labels
and front/back layouts remain intact. Colored contours and cuts share the
existing palettes; bright hats and facial highlights intentionally remain
open under the higher contrast. The original photographed coverings and
portrait proportions are retained. Bleed extensions remain continuous.

Native diagnostics retain `MARKS_REJECTED` and `TONE_UNDERRUN`; no exact
photographic tone match is claimed. Maximum estimated local coverage is
0.785, and native tone RMSE ranges from 0.12038 to 0.30828. These are renderer
diagnostics, not measurements of printed ink.

Final evidence and artifact hashes are in
`examples.data/guilloche-anniversary-24-stage4/validation.json` and `review.json`,
with rendered review evidence in `build/anniversary-24-stage4/pdf-review/`.
No physical printer proof is included.
