This will re-work this page into two types of plans
- "vertical-project" (existing)
- "short" (proposed)


The formal contraints on these two types of plans are:

1. vertical-project
   This is 1080p vertical format video of [1-n] episodes
   - each episode is [1-3] minutes long
   - each series is [60-100] episodes
   - series runtime is (60-150) minutes
   
2. short
  - 1080p, 4k
  - 1 channel
	- landscape
	- portrait
  - 2 channel
    - how channels are arranged together in space
    - side-by-side vertical
	- horizontal grid
	- vertical grid
  - only one video file produced
