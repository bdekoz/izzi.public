# De Kosnik holiday 2026, version 3

## Current direction

The user requested a **reset and complete `holiday-v3` set**, carrying the
new constraints forward from the v2 trials. The output is
`outputs/dk-2026-holiday-v3/`. V2 remains a set of preceding trials; its full
batch was stopped when the composition brief changed.

Produce **fifty designs: ten variations of each of the five full photographs**
in `resources.rizal/de-kosnik-2026`. All cards use a **6 x 4 inch landscape
frame**, 152.4 x 101.6 mm. Each photographic bounding rectangle must occupy
**no more than 61%** of the frame. The earlier 50% ceiling is superseded.

Use the native portrait API on the entire photograph, including clothing,
gestures, architecture, vegetation, lights, bridge and sky. Keep the image's
aspect ratio and EXIF orientation. The photo fades into guilloche that grows
from its composition and background. The photographic dots, lines,
cross-hatching and feature contours are **colored with the same palette as
the guilloche**, rather than defaulting to black.

The final composition corrections are **more asymmetry, more whitespace and
notan**, with **more vibrant hues**. The wild stage-3 interference remains a
reference for complexity, but should occupy selected regions instead of
covering every part of the sheet at equal density.

## Composition system

- Place the complete photo off center, alternating left/right and upper/lower
  emphasis across the ten versions.
- Balance it with an opposing, source-related light or dark mass. Use strong
  value separation and a simple readable silhouette before adding detail.
- Reserve a clear type area in the opposite corner; no dense blanket of
  lines behind all of the lettering.
- Grow guilloche along two corridors from photographed scene landmarks into
  the opposing mass. Use displaced membranes, perspective facets, catenary
  meshes or canopy contours as appropriate to that photograph.
- Retain real quiet paper/ground around the active areas. Dark palettes can
  have quiet dark ground; negative space need not always be literal white.
- Fade native photo marks near their complete source boundary. Reduce
  ornament through the photo interior, then strengthen it through the edge
  transition. No rectangular photo outline or opaque photo plate.

Landscape photo widths vary from 90 to 112 mm, preserving the 4:3 source
ratio. The largest is 112 x 84 mm, approximately 60.76% of the card. H02
keeps its portrait source ratio at 63 x 84 or 69 x 92 mm, within the same
landscape frame. Receipts record the exact bounds per card.

## Palette and engraving variations

Each source gets two of each requested family:

| Versions | Palette family | V3 treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| v01-v02 | Inferred high contrast | Photo-derived hues with stronger saturation; pale and dark grounds |
| v03-v04 | Japanese inspired | Strong indigo/vermilion/ivory and persimmon/moss/blue on warm paper |
| v05-v06 | Neon | Hot magenta/acid green/turquoise and ultraviolet/cyan/electric pink |
| v07-v08 | Blue / white / creme | Brilliant cobalt and blue on porcelain; luminous blue/white/creme on midnight |
| v09-v10 | Red / green / black | Vivid red and green with black accents; bright holly colors on a black ground |

These are authored palette interpretations. The Japanese-inspired schemes
do not claim historical pigment matching. Inferred schemes retain the
source sample provenance while explicitly increasing saturation/value.

The ten mixed-DLH and flow recipes from the v2 plan remain the starting
engraving settings, with contrast 1.65, separate contour emphasis 0.45,
maximum line width 0.16 mm and maximum dot diameter 0.20 mm. Mixtures are
relative tone weights, not percentages of final mark counts. The final
version retains equal thirds with zero flow and zero turn for straight cuts.

Dark-ground versions use a complementary display-luminance preparation for
light ink, preserving a positive tonal reading. This applies to the entire
source image. Original files and complete rectangular native support remain
unchanged. Paint and fading are applied after native geometry generation.

## Text review carried forward

V2 H01-v03 was accepted as a useful reference. The user rejected dark outlines
on the neon lettering, approved a light/white outline, then requested less
width. H02-v06 received an explicit **maximum 0.5 pt** outline limit.

For this fresh v3 set, use a consistent **light outline no wider than 0.5 pt
(0.1763889 mm)**. The quieter type areas should provide most of the separation;
the outline must not become a heavy white border. V2's accepted files remain
available without being overwritten by v3.

## Implementation and deliverables

The v3 source/recipe module, renderer and export adapter are separate from
v2. They reuse the existing native portrait engine and shared whole-photo
preparation. Native geometry is generated at the actual placed physical size.

Completed output in `outputs/dk-2026-holiday-v3/`:

- Fifty SVGs, 600 dpi PNGs and browser previews.
- A local gallery with all original images and ten studies per source.
- Five per-photo contact sheets and an all-fifty overview.
- A ten-page compact comparison PDF and a fifty-page vector print PDF.
- Complete source, color, DLH, flow, fade, placement and field receipts.
- Validation of full-frame input, the 61% ceiling, palette grouping,
  thin light outlines, source geometry, export dimensions and PDF delivery.

```sh
python3 scripts/render-dk-2026-holiday-v3.py --deliver
```
