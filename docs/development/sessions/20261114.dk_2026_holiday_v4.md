The session record was renamed to `20261114.dk_2026_holiday_v4.md` on
November 14. Output, recipe and script names remain `fall-crazy-pants`.

The user liked the **portraiture of the holiday cards** and the **guilloche
and graphic design of the anniversary currency**, and requested a new output
combining them in `fall-crazy-pants`.

## Working design

Output is `outputs/fall-crazy-pants/`. Keep all five holiday photographs and
ten variations each: **fifty designs**. The working format retains the latest
holiday brief's **6 x 4 inch landscape** frame and **2026** wording.

The complete photograph remains the subject, including gestures, clothing,
architecture, vegetation and sky. Native portrait controls, colored layers,
high contrast, flow variations and the 32-step edge fade come from holiday-v3.
No faces are isolated. Every source keeps its orientation and aspect ratio,
and its complete rectangular bounds remain below **61%** of the frame.

The graphic composition follows the anniversary currency: continuous
sheet-wide engraving, phase-shifted crossing fields, displaced moire lenses,
curved color partitions, membranes, a script **De Kosnik** heading, an
oversized **26**, fine double borders and a footer with **2026** and the
greeting. Light text outlines remain **0.5 pt**. The typography and photo
interior receive softer fields for legibility; the remaining sheet carries
the dense currency ornament.

Five currency systems repeat across the ten color/DLH variants: tidal,
woven, sculptural, aurora and intaglio. Surface tension uses the stage3
3x/4x settings. The field anchors are the same photographed landmarks used
for the holiday scene interpretation. The source stage3 field function is
reused directly; its field coordinates adapt to the taller card while its
physical stroke widths remain unchanged. Portrait geometry is never stretched.

The ten vibrant holiday palette families and DLH/flow recipes remain intact.
On dark grounds, the native source preparation uses complementary display
luminance for light ink, as in holiday-v3. Each photo and its currency field
share the same colors.

The photographic rectangle is shifted upward when necessary to leave room
for the footer. H02 uses 63 x 84 mm in every variant, so its complete portrait
frame fits above that band. Five H02 recipes therefore need native geometry
regenerated at the smaller width; all other existing holiday geometries can
be reused when their complete native cache signatures agree.

Line opacity and filled color partitions use the same smooth photo/type
envelope. Filled partitions are clipped to vector isoline rings; no raster
mask, filter or embedded photo is introduced into the SVG artwork.

contact sheets, a ten-page comparison PDF and a fifty-page vector print PDF.

Verify source integrity, whole-photo support, native geometry/settings,
physical dimensions, the 61% limit, thin light outlines, vector-only exports,
gallery links and PDF-to-SVG agreement. Review pilot images before the full
batch, then inspect every comparison page. Earlier editions remain separate.


lettering detail. Bright palettes emphasize the drawn marks; dark palettes

source frames, native settings, colored layers, fades, physical photo bounds,
currency field recipes, lettering clearances, 0.5 pt light outlines, 600 dpi
PNGs, gallery links and PDF dimensions. The print PDF contains no embedded
raster artwork; the comparison PDF is explicitly a raster selection proof.

and their prior independent replay evidence still matches. All five H02
portraits regenerated at 63 mm width passed new independent replays. This
checks every geometry while avoiding redundant regeneration of the other 45.
