### Resume and spending behavior

The current runner permits only segments 1 and 2 per provider. It saves
`request.json` and `submit-intent.json` before a paid POST, then saves the
submission receipt immediately. An existing `submit.json` prevents another
submission. An intent without a receipt stops the runner for reconciliation;
it must not be removed merely to try another paid POST.

`run` polls every 45 seconds with a 30-minute deadline. Failed/cancelled
jobs stop the run; a timeout leaves the job records available to collect
later. Read-only collection may be resumed. A media-download failure does
not require a new generation. HTTP submission errors are recorded without
an automatic paid retry. The full workflow should retain these properties
with unique run/segment identifiers and an explicit total job budget.

## 8. Durable full-episode workflow

The following is the extension contract for full production. It is not an
assertion that the bounded canary runner already implements these stages.
Use a separate production run directory and configuration; preserve the
accepted canary directory and its submission records.

| Stage | Required input and operation | Durable result / completion condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Freeze | Canonical bytes and all 191 turns | Source hash, exact copy, lossless ledger; explicit disposition of every non-dialogue span |
| Dialogue | Stable voices; source-preserving chunks for long turns; record pronunciation overrides | Per-turn/chunk text offsets, voice/weight hashes, seed/settings, WAVs, sample counts, ASR and listening results |
| Timeline | Measure complete synthesized audio before choosing picture duration | Sample-accurate placements with pauses, no clipped or accelerated speech, exact caption alignment |
| Shot plan | Accepted frame/style references; successive canyon, switchback and ridge compositions | Named continuity groups, input frame hashes, explicit seeds/prompts, source/audio ranges, budget |
| Render | Provider adapter with persisted intent and job receipts | Originals, output seeds, measured dimensions/durations, charge records; no uncertain POST repeats |
| Continue | Verify each previous final frame and inspect each short group | Frame-transfer hashes and similarity checks; reset from a prepared route keyframe at planned picture cuts |
| Assemble | Complete audio and approved picture coverage | Native 1080×1920/24 fps video, PCM master, exact subtitle sidecars, captioned review MP4 |
| Validate and accept | Source-to-audio-to-caption coverage plus geometry and continuity checks | Technical results separate from human review, each bound to exact artifact hashes |

Split synthesis input at engine-safe boundaries while retaining contiguous
offsets into each original turn. Concatenated chunk text must reconstruct
that turn exactly; word counts alone cannot detect compensating omissions
and duplications. Check all generated chunks for truncation. Regenerate a
faulty performance using the same words rather than editing the transcript.

Base the audio timeline on decoded sample counts. For the full episode,
picture coverage must reach the final dialogue sample. Dialogue may cross
picture cuts. Keep excess picture handles or pad picture when necessary;
do not shorten the audio to fit a nominal clip boundary. Captions must
continue through the last utterance.

Two adjacent 15-second segments are proven. Start production with similarly
short continuity groups and inspect before extending; hundreds of blind
last-frame continuations are not validated by this canary. Introduce new
route keyframes at planned picture cuts while preserving the four-animal
cast and visual grammar. Store every change to prompts, frames, voices,
seeds, or provider configuration as an explicit revision.

Each production run manifest should contain at least:

- Run/baseline IDs, code revision/hash, source hash and ledger version,
  runtime versions, exact asset paths/hashes, and full audio duration.
- Speaker casting, pronunciation map, text/chunk offsets, native and
  delivery sample rates, audio placements and caption text/alignment.
- Per-segment provider, endpoint, exact body and its serialization/hash,
  input frame/hash, seed requested/returned, continuity-group ID, and
  planned picture/audio ranges.
- Submission intent, idempotency key where supported, provider job ID,
  status/result receipts, timestamps, charges/refunds, original media hash,
  probe results, and final-frame transfer provenance.
- Delivery filenames/hashes, technical checks, human feedback verbatim,
  review scope, and the exact artifact hashes accepted.

The accepted canaries include sustained albatross flight despite the prompt's
low-hop instruction. fal changes background compositions faster; Seedance
holds the canyon view longer. Magenta flowers sometimes overlap the cat's
silhouette. These are recorded traits of visually accepted results, not
reasons to reopen their acceptance. Inspect later generations for actual
species/limb drift, lost markings, disruptive cuts, or style changes.
