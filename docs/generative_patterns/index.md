# Generative pattern families

The section index lives at [generative pattern families](../generative_patterns.md).

The [examples workflows](../development/examples-workflows.md) build
independent books of native parameter studies and authored compositions.
Film, VCR, TV and satellite use the same catalog through the
[screen media facade](../generative_movement/screen-glitch.md).

The [native guilloche patterns](../generative_patterns.md#native-guilloche-patterns)
guide, construction references and visual comparisons are integrated into that
top-level document. It covers asymmetric contours, basketweave, hobnail,
flinqué, foliate and botanical forms, coral/anemones, Islamic geometric
arabesque, graphic water, optical controls and lenticular export.

- [hamonshu](hamonshu.md) — canonical API, native water fields, complete
  three-volume graphic-water catalogue, provenance and revision map
- [guilloche](guilloche.md) — nine accepted capability families plus P1-P5

  the Stage 4 Dutch lighthouse radiate review plate, and six gated Stage 5
  Dutch multi-motif 3-by-5 review plates, the Stage 6 security panel, and the
  Stage 7 vector master, scalloped medallion, ten wild variations and full script OTF
- [moire](moire.md) — nine mathematical field kinds plus M1-M3 tuning,
  hybrid composition, degeneration, and render-profile coverage notes
- [danmu](danmu.md) — danmaku text overlay family incl. the W3C Bullet
