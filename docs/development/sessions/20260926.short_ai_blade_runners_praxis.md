  scaled uniformly and cropped around its center to fit a face, eye, or mouth
  region without stretching the guilloche geometry. The environmental layer
  spans the frame; the face layer supplies the moving linework. Source-SVG and
  derived-layer hashes are in the production manifest.
- A virtual vertical camera pans from face to the far horizontal edge before
  each source cut, then from the opposite edge to the next face after that
  cut. Separate variable 1–3 s moves are eased and shortened when a shot is
  too brief. Cut frames, crop positions, plate switches, and pan windows are
  in each picture tracking receipt. ARX2's dark/profile shots use reviewed
  shot-specific face anchors where MediaPipe loses detection, including
  Leon's monitor image. Tracking receipts report zero frames without a tracked
  face region for placing linework.
- Both films have 26 SVG selection intervals. ARX1 has five cut-aware pan
  pairs; ARX2 has eight. Their receipts record the exact plate pages/hashes,
  switch frames, cut frames, pan windows, and per-frame crop position.

## Speech and mix

- Higgs TTS 2 3B ran on `eureka` using the Here Lies Trouble rev-2 reference
  bank. Character voices use V01/V02/V03 consistently. Anomalous ARX2 unit
  03 was regenerated; ARX2 unit 01 was regenerated to articulate “off-world.”
  Seeds, hashes, accepted retries, and reference hashes are recorded in
  `outputs/ai-runner/tts/accepted-generation.json`.
- Rubber Band fits long units to their original speech slots. Short units
  keep a natural pause instead of stretching across the entire slot.
  ARX1 unit 02 has an editable end trim at 3.72 s to remove a generated
  trailing “and” after “Orion”; this was found in the final-mix ASR pass.
  Dialogue targets −18 LUFS. The stereo background stem is mixed from the
  7.1 source's rear surround channels (BL/SL and BR/SR) at −32 LUFS. This
  keeps the source rain and room tone under the new dialogue while reducing
  the original speech; the bed ducks under each generated line. The final
  48 kHz stereo mix targets −16 LUFS-I and at most −1.5 dBTP. ARX1 has gentle
  compression to reach that target without peak limiting artifacts. Its
  final six seconds of rain/room tone comes from the preceding clean ambience
  segment, crossfaded over 46.0–46.4 s, because the source actor's final line
  otherwise leaked into the rear channels.
- Whisper large-v3-turbo re-transcribed both mixes. It recovered the key
  edited lines, including “Time to die,” “off-world colonies,” and the
  corrected aerial patrol line. The word-level comparison is in
  asr-diff.json: 0.899
  sequence match for ARX1 and 0.965 for ARX2. The ARX1 full-file ASR repeats
  “Time to die” near the end, but separate ASR of the last 3.8 s of the final
  mix produces no words (only a dash), and separate ASR of the rain/room-tone
  stem identifies music rather than speech. The repeat appears to be a
  context-driven ASR error. Other differences include “C”/“sea,” “aerial
  patrol”/“aeropatrol,” “field”/“fields,” “Voight-Kampff”/“voykampff,” an
  inserted “um,” “one”/“once,” and a missing “Well.” These warrant human
  listening review; ASR is evidence, not sign-off.
