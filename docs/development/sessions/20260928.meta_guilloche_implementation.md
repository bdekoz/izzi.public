## Visual disposition and remaining external checks

The implementation review inspected core, engraving, arabesque, botanical,
reef, water, optical, attachment and motion sheets at full and small sizes.
It prompted real geometry changes: separated swirl rings, larger foliate
curls, reduced narrow-leaf veins, hidden lettuce/bubble edges, continuous
fox folds, rounded/warped brain valleys and tentacles across the carpet disc.
Dense limit plates intentionally demonstrate loss of separation at small
sizes; the guide explains that they are exploration limits.

Human aesthetic acceptance remains `PENDING` in authored examples.
Perceptual strength is not established by equal-coordinate controls.
**Physical lenticular lens/print proof was not performed**; the packets
include pitch/ruler/alignment artifacts for that separate hardware step.
The girih example is an original finite five-tile patch, not a claim of
historical reproduction or quasiperiodic tiling. Botanical/reef forms are
stylized constructions rather than species-identification diagrams.

[Generative pattern families](../../generative_patterns.md#native-guilloche-patterns).

and preserves the surrounding prose. The classification JSON remains the
data source; crops, HTML browsers and SVG/JSON review artifacts remain
derived resources. The former standalone catalogue document was removed.
