### What changes and what must be built

Keep the complete fourth draft, all four stock voices, existing master
audio, accepted illustrated terrain and approved wildlife treatment.
Stage 5 must make the speaking animal visibly speak and the two intended
animals visibly look at each other. Generic head motion, a look toward the
camera, or a provider status of `completed` does not pass this requirement.

The main extra work is reusable facial animation and its timing:

1. **Four animal-specific face controls.** Prepare rest/open mouth shapes
   and explicit forward/partner-facing head and eye poses. Use a restrained
   jaw/lip movement for the unicorn, dog and cat, and a hinged beak for the
   albatross. Preserve their markings and anatomy. Track these controls to
   the walking heads with masks for occlusion; simply drawing a moving mouth
   over an untracked face will slide. The first albatross/dog local rig is
   built; the other three speaking mouths and tracking onto generated
   moving animals remain unimplemented.
2. **Speech-driven timing.** Read the frozen speaker/chunk timeline and
   animate only the current speaker's mouth from its actual audio. Start
   with smoothed speech activity and syllabic energy, including closures
   during pauses. If closer articulation is needed, align phonemes to the
   cached speech and map them to a small set of mouth shapes. Speech-timed
   opening/closing is the initial target; it is not a claim of exact
   phoneme-level lip synchronization. Listeners' mouths rest during silence.
3. **Readable paired poses.** Stage the active pair so both faces remain
   visible and large enough to read at normal viewing size. Use a clear
   partner-facing head turn, with eyes leading where the drawing supports
   it. A starting design target is roughly 20–35 degrees of head rotation,
   a 0.4–0.6-second transition, a returned look about 0.25 seconds later,
   and a 1.5-second mutual hold before attention returns to the path.
   These are proposed controls to test, not measured Seedance behavior.
   Keep the walk gentle and avoid wildlife reactions during the first proof.
4. **A new Stage 5 runner and manifest.** Store exact source/audio/pose hashes,
   per-speaker mouth intervals, gaze targets, prompts, input mode, quoted
   rate, task ID, charges, review evidence and approval. Reuse the existing
   submission ledger, fresh-balance checks and duplicate-request protection.
   Do not modify the Stage 4 frozen manifests or historical prompts. Remove
   the old blanket mouth-motion restriction from the new prompt and replace
   it with explicit active-speaker animation and listener rest instructions.
