contains the 20 `film/` algorithms. Beta's deck contains the 18 video
algorithms: six `vcr/`, six `tv-broadcast/`, and six `satellite/` algorithms.

The proposed full sequence ends when the shorter beta deck is exhausted:

algorithms. The other two film algorithms remain unused. This stopping
rule is an implementation proposal for review, since the human did not
specify how to resolve the different deck lengths. Never wrap either deck

Adapt the approved draft 4 timing to the two requested layers:

| Relative time | State |
| --- | --- |
| 0–6 seconds | Alpha alone |
| 6–12 seconds | Keep the same alpha; add beta above it |
| 12–14 seconds | Keep beta; fade alpha out over two seconds |
| 14–16 seconds | Fade beta out over two seconds |
| After beta exits | Canonical video alone for a seeded random 2–6 seconds, then the next pair |

Each instance persists from entry through its exit. Its native clock does
not restart when another layer enters or exits. The final pair ends when

full-range sRGB with BT.709 primaries, matching the SVG compositor. Record
the original-file checksum and extraction settings. Do not reuse the old

## Color and image quality

Use the exact blue and red requested by the human, replacing the old
reference-calibrated CMY palette. Keep one continuous subtractive-style
coverage equation. For ink coverages `a` and `b`, weights are `a(1-b)` for
alpha alone, `(1-a)b` for beta alone, and `ab` for their overlap. Compute
the overlap color with component-wise multiplication of the two normalized
RGB colors. For these exact blue and red inks, complete overlap is black;
this follows the general formula and has no pair-specific override.
Total ink coverage is `1-(1-a)(1-b)`, composited over canonical footage.
Zero coverage retains the footage. Fade the native coverage, not a moving
rectangle or an independently restarted glitch.

Use the approved native effect-versus-source coverage extraction with
uniform gain 2. Film material's native exposure carrier only determines
coverage; it is not the visible background. Actual canonical video
history supplies the video glitch algorithms. Preserve aspect ratio and
the existing vertical cover crop, without stretching.

Review at **1080 × 1920, 30 fps**, rasterizing SVG through Inkscape at twice
the output dimensions and downsampling with Lanczos. After approval, use
**2160 × 3840, 30 fps** with the same supersampling for the full vertical
production. Preserve RGB losslessly in an FFV1 level 3 MKV, with 24-bit,
48 kHz FLAC from the approved normal-speed rain/thunder audio. Verify the
MKV against every raster frame's RGB hash and verify its audio samples.

The review viewing copy is 10-bit HEVC, x265 slow CRF 14, in MP4. Convert
full-range sRGB to correctly tagged limited-range BT.709 before encoding.
The eventual YouTube copy uses x265 veryslow two-pass HEVC, AAC 384 kbps,
and a target of 985,000,000 bytes within the requested 0.5–1.0 GB range.
The size target applies to the approved full upload, not the 60-second
review or the lossless master. Decode and verify the complete viewing
copy, including frame count, duration, dimensions, tags, and checksum.
