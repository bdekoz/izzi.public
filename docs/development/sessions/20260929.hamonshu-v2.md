### Remaining work and how to resume

1. Collect the human category JSON files or inspect the user's named local
   directories. Review crop-revision notes and choose the human return to apply
   for each book; do not infer categories or merge reviewers automatically.
2. Preview a selected return with
   `python3 scripts/apply-hamonshu-categories.py /path/to/review.json`.
   Its volume selects the local packet. Apply the selected review with `--apply`,
   then run `python3 scripts/prepare-hamonshu-crops.py --volume N --refresh-index`
   for that book. The importer archives the original return, including notes
   and reviewer identity, under `_review/category-reviews/`.
3. Continue the unfinished portions of stages 1–3: use human-defined categories
   to synthesize faithful SVG constructions, infer useful parameters, and build
   parameter grids. The original brief specifically calls for variable-width
   lines, polygons, complex paths, and other SVG elements.
4. Complete stage 4's approach/API documentation in the canonical guide, then
   stage 5's 10 × 10 wave-guilloché experiment grid.

The category importer supports Books 1–3 and rejects another volume, a changed
plan, or mixed-book IDs even when `--output-dir` is supplied. Preparation and
publication accept `--volume 1`, `2`, or `3`; Book 1 remains the default.
