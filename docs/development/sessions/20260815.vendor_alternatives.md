## 1. The short answer

**izzi cannot run "the same model" on eureka or ord from Hugging Face,
because the model izzi's vendor resells has no public weights.** ByteDance
has not released Seedance 2.0 (or 2.5) weights. The official ByteDance
Hugging Face organizations carry no Seedance checkpoints, no redistribution
license exists, and the official model page routes to the Dreamina app and
the paid enterprise API only
([reapi.ai, 2026-07-02](https://reapi.ai/blog/can-you-run-seedance-locally);
[seedance2pro.io, 2026-06-07](https://seedance2pro.io/es/blog/seedance-2-open-source-local-guide)).
`seedance2ai.io` is an independent reseller (SixBryan LLC) of that API-only
model (`model: "seedance2"` in every observed response), not a weights
provider.

What Hugging Face *does* offer is a different family of open video models —
Wan 2.2/2.6, HunyuanVideo, LTX-Video, CogVideoX, Mochi — and izzi's two
worker hosts are the wrong shape for the ones that approach Seedance-class
output quality. Section 3 walks the hardware math.

## 2. Why a vendor at all

Even before comparing prices, the vendor path earns its place on these
grounds, all of which izzi has now exercised in production:

- **Model parity.** The desired model is only reachable through an API. Local
  open models are a substitute, not the same model, and the substitute is
  one or more quality generations behind for the stylized-animation work
  izzi is producing.
- **Zero operations.** No GPU to buy, mount, cool, driver-pin, or swap; no
  ROCm nightly churn; no model card, safetensors download, VAE, or
  offload-tuning; no queueing or job manager to build. izzi already proved
  the cost of self-hosting on gfx1151 during the Higgs TTS work
  ([audio-higgs.md](../seedance/audio-higgs.md)).
- **Throughput at izzi's volume.** The API completed every 10 s job in
  roughly 2–5 minutes end to end. That is faster than any realistic
  iGPU-bound local run (section 3) and requires no supervision.
- **A usable API contract.** Bearer auth, `Idempotency-Key`, task polling,
  CDN download URLs, `credits_used`/`credits_refunded` on every response.
  The adapter scripts in
  `build/private/seedance2-submit-one.sh`
  rely on this.
- **Cost fits the workload.** izzi's measured burn is ~6,087 credits across
  36 jobs (see the companion
  [audit](20260815.vendor_audit_seedance2ai.md)); at the
  annual-plan credit values that is roughly $50–75 total (or ≈ $173 at
  one-time Starter Pack prices). That is well below the engineering and
  electricity cost of standing up a local-generation stack, and below the
  monthly cost of any dedicated GPU box.

The vendor model's real risks are the ones izzi already recorded against
`seedance2ai.io` specifically: no published rate card, no balance/quote
endpoint, and a persistent resolution cap (1080p requested → 720×1280
delivered; 720p requested → 496×864). Those are *vendor* defects, not
arguments against vendors in general. The alternatives in section 4 fix most
of them.

### 3.3 Why gfx1151 is the wrong engine for this job

- **Memory bandwidth.** Strix Halo's Radeon 8060S shares ~256 GB/s of unified
  memory bandwidth. Diffusion-video sampling is bandwidth-bound; a modern
  data-center GPU (A100/H100-class) has 1.5–3+ TB/s. Community Strix Halo
  measurements put a 10 s, 480×832, 121-frame clip at **~10–13 minutes**,
  and HunyuanVideo at 5–8 minutes per clip after the
  `HSA_USE_SVM=0` fix ([TheRock #2684](https://github.com/ROCm/TheRock/discussions/2684),
  [AMD Strix Halo AI Guide](https://github.com/bkpaine1/AMD-Strix-Halo-AI-Guide)).
  Scaling that to 720×1280 (izzi's minimum viable delivery) and then to
  native 1080×1920 puts a single clip at tens of minutes to hours, with
  queued jobs serializing behind each other.
- **Stability.** gfx1151 ROCm is usable but fragile: community reports of
  bf16 correctness bugs, memory-op hangs, and the `HSA_USE_SVM` workaround
  are already recorded in izzi's Higgs investigation. Production video
  batches would inherit that risk.
- **Capacity is already assigned.** eureka runs Higgs TTS v2 (≈11 GB of
  weights) and ord is the audio-encode worker. Repurposing either for video
  generation would serialize against the audio pipeline izzi needs for
  episodes, and neither has the 24–80 GB *GPU-class* memory that the
  quality-tier open models want.
- **Engineering tax.** Even at 480p-class output, local means building and
  maintaining a ComfyUI/vLLM/ROCm stack, quantizing and pinning weights,
  writing a job queue, and babysitting thermal/ROCm behavior — the exact
  work the vendor path removes.

### 3.4 When local would win

Local open models become the right call only when one of these is true:

- a sustained pipeline generates hundreds to thousands of clips per month
  (amortizing a dedicated 24–80 GB NVIDIA GPU and its ops time);
- the project requires data that cannot leave the LAN (recorded voices,
  unredacted transcripts, licensed reference media);
- a *small* open model's aesthetic is deliberately chosen as the house style
  (LTX-2B-class at 480–576p is the realistic Strix Halo envelope today).

None of those apply to the current 10–60-episode vertical plan, which needs
9:16, 10 s, 720p-class-or-better clips at a few dozen per episode, with no
private media leaving the project.

## 7. Recommendation

1. **Keep vendor rendering, but move the production adapter to fal.ai.**
   It is the only evaluated option that can return *native 1080×1920* from
   the actual Seedance 2.0 model at a published price, with no subscription
   floor. Expected cost: ~$6.82 per 10 s 1080p Seedance clip, or $0.75–1.50
   on the Wan 2.6 route.
2. **Keep `seedance2ai.io` only as a frozen historical adapter** with the
   existing evidence; do not extend it until the resolution cap and billing
   transparency are resolved (see the companion audit's support questions).
3. **Do not self-host on eureka/ord for this project.** Open-model quality
   at izzi's resolution target is not reachable on gfx1151 at production
   throughput, the machines already carry the audio pipeline, and the
   engineering cost exceeds the measured vendor spend. Revisit only with a
   dedicated 24–80 GB NVIDIA GPU and a sustained ≥100-clips/month workload.
4. **Before any switch:** run one 5 s, 9:16, 1080p canary on fal.ai,
   download and `ffprobe` the original, require exactly 1080×1920, record
   the charged amount, and only then port the adapter. Per the 2026-08-15
   decision, that canary is **not authorized** until the `seedance2ai.io`
   support review completes and support responds.
